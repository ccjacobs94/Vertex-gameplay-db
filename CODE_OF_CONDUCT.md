# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community and reliability of self-hosted users' data
- Preserving user-owned library edits and keeping tests offline and reproducible

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information (or real database snapshots/keys) without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may clarify standards and take corrective action (comment removal, warning, or repository access removal) in response to unacceptable behavior. Report concerns privately to the maintainers.
